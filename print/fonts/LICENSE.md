# DejaVu Sans (bundled font)

The TTF files in this directory are from the **DejaVu Sans 2.37** family,
distributed under the [DejaVu Fonts License](https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html)
(a permissive Bitstream Vera–derived license).

Bundled here so `scripts/build-grammar-pdfs.py` can embed them and render
IPA phonetic symbols (/ɪ/, /iː/, /ʃ/, /θ/, …) and Vietnamese diacritics
(ơ, ư, ế, ề, ộ, …) correctly in the print/grammar/*.pdf study sheets.

- DejaVuSans.ttf            (regular)
- DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf       (bold)
- DejaVuSans-Oblique.ttf    (italic)
- DejaVuSans-BoldOblique.ttf (bold italic)

Source: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/
